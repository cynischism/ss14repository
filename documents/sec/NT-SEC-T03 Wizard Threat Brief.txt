                           [bold]NANOTRASEN CORPORATION
               SECURITY DEPARTMENT - SPACE STATION 14[/color][/bold]
─────────────────────────────────────────
[bold]CLASSIFICATION: [color=#cb0000]LEVEL 4 – EYES ONLY[/color][/bold]
─────────────────────────────────────────
[head=3][bold]THREAT OVERVIEW[/bold][/head]
Wizards are spellcasters affiliated with the Wizard Federation, an organization with unclear motives and inconsistent behavior. Individual wizards range from benign wanderers to catastrophic threats capable of mass destruction. Their abilities vary widely, but all wizards possess the potential to inflict severe structural, biological, or temporal damage to the station.

Wizards are not inherently hostile; however, their presence alone constitutes posture requiring immediate monitoring and containment readiness.
─────────────────────────────────────────
[head=3][bold]IDENTIFICATION MARKERS[/bold][/head]
[bullet] Robed individuals wielding staves, grimoires, or arcane tools.  
[bullet] Sudden manifestation of anomalous effects (teleportation, polymorphs, lightning).  
[bullet] Unregistered personnel appearing aboard the station without transit logs.  
[bullet] Speech patterns referencing “Federation,” “the Academy,” or “the Arcane Arts.”  
[bullet] Spontaneous environmental distortions or summoned entities.
─────────────────────────────────────────
[head=3][bold]BEHAVIORAL PROFILE[/bold][/head]
[bullet] Behavior varies from curious and cooperative to openly destructive.  
[bullet] May engage in conversation, trade, or harmless demonstrations.
[bullet] Displays unpredictable shifts in temperament or objectives.  
[bullet] Often tests station response times or personnel reactions.  
[bullet] Capable of fleeing, teleporting, or vanishing without warning.
─────────────────────────────────────────
[head=3][bold]THREAT CAPABILITIES[/bold][/head]

[bold]Spellcasting[/bold]  
[bullet] Wizards possess a wide range of spells, including teleportation, elemental attacks, polymorphs, illusions, and temporal manipulation.

[bold]Summoning[/bold]  
[bullet] Capable of summoning hostile creatures, constructs, or environmental hazards.

[bold]Self‑Augmentation[/bold]  
[bullet] May enhance their own durability, speed, or offensive power through arcane means.

[bold]Spatial Manipulation[/bold]  
[bullet] Can bypass secure areas, escape custody, or relocate rapidly.

[bold]Federation Equipment[/bold]  
[bullet] Staves, wands, scrolls, and artifacts with unpredictable effects.
─────────────────────────────────────────
[head=3][bold]RECOMMENDED RESPONSE[/bold][/head]
[bullet] Attempt peaceful contact if the wizard is non‑hostile; maintain caution.  
[bullet] Monitor their movements and restrict access to critical systems.  
[bullet] Prepare for rapid escalation; keep a response team on standby.  
[bullet] Use ranged weapons and avoid close‑quarters engagement.  
[bullet] Prioritize containment over elimination.
[bullet] Evacuate civilians from areas affected by anomalous activity.
─────────────────────────────────────────
[head=3][bold]AUTHORIZED EQUIPMENT[/bold][/head]
[bullet] Lethal weaponry.  
[bullet] Portable flashers and crowd‑control equipment.  
[bullet] Hazard gear for anomalous environments.
