[head=3][bold]THREAT OVERVIEW[/bold][/head]
A Changeling is a xenoform infiltrator capable of full biometric mimicry, including voice, appearance, and limited memory replication. These entities operate independently of known Syndicate command structures and exhibit adaptive, survival‑driven behavior. Their objectives remain unclear; observed patterns suggest infiltration, study, and potential hive‑linked activity. They are NOT crew, and are not permitted trials.
─────────────────────────────────────────
[head=3][bold]IDENTIFICATION MARKERS[/bold][/head]
• Sudden behavioral drift or personality inconsistencies.
• Unexplained resilience or rapid recovery from trauma.
• Personnel “returning” after unverified disappearance. 
• Biological residue, digestive fluids, or anomalous tissue at attack sites.
• Discovery of partially devoured, or "hollowed", corpses.
─────────────────────────────────────────
[head=3][bold]BEHAVIORAL PROFILE[/bold][/head]
• Prefers stealth, substitution, and isolation of targets.
• Avoids open conflict unless cornered or discovered.
• Capable of imitating victims with high accuracy.
• Demonstrates strategic patience and opportunistic feeding.
─────────────────────────────────────────
[head=3][bold]THREAT CAPABILITIES[/bold][/head]
• Full biometric mimicry (voice, retina, fingerprints).  
• Extreme regenerative capability; resilient to standard weapons.
• Biological weaponry (stingers, bone blades, chemical secretions). 
• Ability to impersonate personnel without leaving a corpse.
• Potential hive‑linked intelligence sharing.
─────────────────────────────────────────
[head=3][bold]RECOMMENDED RESPONSE[/bold][/head]
• Isolate individuals showing cognitive drift or abnormal resilience.
• Avoid public announcements; civilian panic compromises containment.
• Engage only with numerical advantage and controlled environment.
• Terminate via incineration; standard execution methods insufficient.
─────────────────────────────────────────
[head=3][bold]AUTHORIZED EQUIPMENT[/bold][/head]
• Lethal weaponry
• Flamers or incendiary tools  
• Cremation facilities or disposal units  
• Biohazard containment gear  
• Tracking implants for suspected personnel
