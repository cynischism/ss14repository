# Corpsman's Shopping List

Essential mixes for any nuclear corpsman.

[head=3]MEDICINES[/head]
[bullet]3 Hydrogen + 1 Nitrogen = 4 Ammonia
[bullet]1 Ammonia + 1 Table Salt + 1 Carbon + 1 Oxygen = 4 Sodium Carbonate
[bullet]1 Silicon + 1 Carbon = 2 Kelotane
[bullet]1 Kelotane + 1 Oxygen + 1 Phosphorus = 3 Dermaline
[bullet]1 Oxygen + 1 Hydrogen + >310K = 2 Hydroxide
[bullet]1 Hydrogen + 1 Carbon + >310K = 1 Benzene
[bullet]1 Hydroxide + 1 Benzene = 2 Phenol
[bullet]1 Iron + 1 Silicon + >310K = 2 Fersilicite
[bullet]1 Copper + 1 Fersilicite + 1 Plasma(cat.) = 2 Leporazine
[bold]1 Sodium Carbonate + 1 Dermaline + 1 Phenol + 1 Leporazine
= 4 Oxandrolone[/bold]
[italic]Heals 4 burn and 3 caustic over 25 damage, 1 otherwise. OD over 25u.[/italic]

[bullet]1 Oxygen + 1 Carbon + 1 Sugar = 3 Inaprovaline
[bullet]2 Oxygen + 1 Hydrogen + 1 Sulfur = 3 Sulfuric Acid
[bullet]1 Inaprovaline + 1 Sulfuric Acid + 1 Sugar = 3 Tranexamic Acid
[bullet]1 Fluorine + 1 Hydrogen + 1 Potassium + 1 Sulfuric Acid = 4 Fluorosulfuric Acid
[bullet]1 Oxygen + 1 Hydrogen + >310K = 2 Hydroxide
[bullet]1 Hydroxide + 1 Sodium = 2 Sodium Hydroxide
[bullet] 1 Inaprovaline + 1 Carbon = 2 Bicaridine
[bold]1 Tranexamic Acid + 1 Fluorosulfuric Acid + 1 Sodium Hydroxide + 1 Bicaridine + >370K
= 4 Salicylic Acid[/bold]
[italic]Heals 4 blunt over 25 damage, 1 otherwise. OD over 25u.[/italic]

[bullet]4 Water + 1 Table Salt = 5 Saline
[bullet]1 Oxygen + 1 Carbon + 1 Sugar = 3 Inaprovaline
[bullet]2 Oxygen + 1 Hydrogen + 1 Sulfur = 3 Sulfuric Acid
[bullet]1 Inaprovaline + 1 Sulfuric Acid + 1 Sugar = 3 Tranexamic Acid
[bold]3 Saline + 2 Tranexamic Acid + 1 Iron + 1 Copper + >310K
= 12 Artiplates[/bold]
[italic]Reduces bleeding, increases blood, and heals 5 bloodloss. OD over 11u.[/italic]

[bullet/]3 Hydrogen + 1 Nitrogen = 4 Ammonia
[bullet/]1 Ammonia + 1 Ethanol = 2 Diethylamine
[bullet/]1 Welding Fuel + 1 Hydrogen + 1 Carbon = 3 Oil
[bullet/]1 Oil + 1 Hydrogen + 1 Sugar + 1 Diethylamine = 4 Ephedrine
[bold]1 Ephedrine + 1 Vestine + 2 Oxygen + >370K
= 2 Hyperzine[/bold]
[italic]Combat stimulant. Heals 0.25 burn and 0.33 brute over 70 damage. Buffs movement speed by 1.3x, heals 10 stamina damage, suppresses pain, causes adrenanaline, and cancels stun. OD at 20u.[/italic]

[bullet]1 Welding Fuel + 1 Hydrogen + 1 Carbon = 3 Oil

[bullet]1 Iron + 1 Aluminum + 1 Oxygen

[italic]Increases fire temperature.[/italic]
