[head=3]EXPLOSIVES[/head]
1 Potassium + 1 Water
= Basic explosive
[italic]Explodes on reagent contact.[/italic]

1 Aluminum + 1 Iron + 1 Uranium
= EMP mix
[italic]Shorts out electronics.[/italic]

1 Aluminum + 1 Potassium + 1 Sulfur
= Flashbang mix
[italic]Stuns and blinds like a flash.[/italic]

[bullet/]1 Chlorine + 3 Fluorine + >370K
= 4 Chlorine Trifluoride
[italic]Explodes violently when combined. Likely to kill anyone at its center and crit anyone in a tight radius. Use in a cryostasis beaker for an unpleasant grenade.[/italic]

[bullet/]1 Phosphorus + 1 Sulfuric Acid + 1 Plasma
= 3 Phlogiston
[italic]Ignites the target. Combine with thermite or napalm to increase the burn damage.[/italic]

[bullet/]1 Charcoal + 1 Saltpeter + 1 Sulfur
= 3 Black Powder
[italic]A primitive but powerful explosive. Explodes at 474K.[/italic]

[bullet/]1 Ammonia + 1 Nitric Acid = 2 Ammonium Nitrate
[bullet/]2 Ammonium Nitrate + 1 Welding Fuel
= 2 Ammonium Nitrate fuel oil
[italic]A relatively basic blasting agent. Explodes at 576K.[/italic]

[bullet/]1 Nitrogen + 1 Phosphorus + 1 Potassium = 3 EZ Nutrient
[bullet/]1 Potassium + 1 Chlorine + 1 Sulfuric Acid = 2 Potash
[bullet/]1 EZ Nutrient + 1 Potash + 1 Nitrogen = 2 Saltpeter + 1 Water
[bullet/]2 Saltpeter + 1 Sulfuric Acid + >357K = 2 Nitric Acid + 1 Potassium Sulfate
[bullet/]1 Carbon + 1 Oxygen + >773K = 2 Carbon Dioxide
[bullet/]4 Hydrogen + 1 Carbon Dioxide + 1 Aluminum (cat.) = 3 Methane + 2 Water
[bullet/]1 Methane + 1 Water + 1 Oxygen = 3 Methanol
[bullet/]1 Methanol + 1 Oxygen + 1 Silver (cat.) + >357K = 1 Formaldehyde + 1 Water
[bullet/]3 Formaldehyde + 2 Ammonia = 5 Hexamine
[bullet/]1 Hexamine + 1 Nitric Acid
= 2 Cyclonite
[italic]The explosive component of plastic explosives. Explodes at 576K.[/italic]
