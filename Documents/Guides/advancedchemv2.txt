# Advanced Chemistry guide

This is a rapid walkthrough for creating T3 medicines intended for chemists who don't have the recipes and procedures memorized. This may not be the most efficient guide, but it will ensure that you produce enough medicine in enough time for most emergency situations.

Additional recipes are provided at the bottom of this guide. Note that any recipe requiring blood specifically requires red blood - insect, blue, ammoniated, and synth blood will not work!

[head=3]MAJOR[/head]
[bullet/]4 Water + 1 Table Salt = 5 Saline
[bullet/]1 Oxygen + 1 Carbon + 1 Sugar = 3 Inaprovaline
[bullet/]2 Oxygen + 1 Hydrogen + 1 Sulfur = 3 Sulfuric Acid
[bullet/]1 Inaprovaline + 1 Sulfuric Acid + 1 Sugar = 3 Tranexamic Acid
[bold]3 Saline + 2 Tranexamic Acid + 1 Iron + 1 Copper + >310K
= 12 Artiplates[/bold]
[italic]Reduces bleeding, increases blood, and heals 5 bloodloss. OD over 11u.[/italic]

= 4 Atropine[/bold]
[italic]Increases blood, reduces bleeding, heals 2 asphyx, 0.66 brute, 0.5 burn, 1 poison, and 1 radiation. Best used on crit patients. OD over 35u.[/italic]
