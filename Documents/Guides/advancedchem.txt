# Advanced Chemistry guide

This is a rapid walkthrough for creating T3 medicines intended for chemists who don't have the recipes and procedures memorized. This may not be the most efficient guide, but it will ensure that you produce enough medicine in enough time for most emergency situations.

Additional recipes are provided at the bottom of this guide. Note that any recipe requiring blood specifically requires red blood - insect, blue, ammoniated, and synth blood will not work!

[head=3]EXPLOSIVES[/head]
1 Potassium + 1 Water
= Basic explosive
[italic]Explodes on reagent contact.[/italic]

1 Aluminum + 1 Iron + 1 Uranium
= EMP mix
[italic]Shorts out electronics.[/italic]

1 Aluminum + 1 Potassium + 1 Sulfur
= Flashbang mix
[italic]Stuns and blinds like a flash.[/italic]

[bullet/]1 Chlorine + 3 Fluorine + >370K
= 4 Chlorine Trifluoride
[italic]Explodes violently when combined. Likely to kill anyone at its center and crit anyone in a tight radius. Use in a cryostasis beaker for an unpleasant grenade.[/italic]

[bullet/]1 Phosphorus + 1 Sulfuric Acid + 1 Plasma
