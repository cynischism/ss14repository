                           [bold]NANOTRASEN CORPORATION
               SECURITY DEPARTMENT - SPACE STATION 14[/color][/bold]

[bold]CLASSIFICATION: [color=#cb0000]LEVEL 4 – EYES ONLY[/color][/bold]
─────────────────────────────────────────
[head=3][bold]THREAT OVERVIEW[/bold][/head]
The Cosmic Cult is a conversion‑based extremist group devoted to extra‑dimensional intelligences collectively referred to as “the Unknown.” Cultists siphon a metaphysical resource known as *Entropy* from living crew, using it to empower a central structure called the *Monument*. Once fully empowered, the cult can perform catastrophic rituals capable of destabilizing the station, subverting artificial intelligence, or summoning large‑scale anomalies.

Their doctrine emphasizes inevitability, fatalism, and the “gentle ending” of all things. Cultists are highly coordinated, share progress collectively, and grow exponentially more dangerous as their Monument gains power.
─────────────────────────────────────────
[head=3][bold]IDENTIFICATION MARKERS[/bold][/head]
[bullet] References to “the Unknown,” “the Curtain,” or “Entropy”.
[bullet] Possession of runes, glyphs, or ritual markings.
[bullet] Trance‑like behavior, dissociation, or sudden ideological shifts.
[bullet] Unexplained health decay in crew following close contact.
[bullet] Strange structures or symbols appearing in maintenance or secluded areas.
─────────────────────────────────────────
[head=3][bold]BEHAVIORAL PROFILE[/bold][/head]
[bullet] Avoids unnecessary killing; prefers keeping crew alive for Entropy extraction.
[bullet] Operates in cells, coordinating silently via anomalous communication.
[bullet] Rapidly converts crew once the Monument is active.
[bullet] Defends empowered structures with fanatic resolve.
[bullet] Escalates from subtle manipulation to open ritual activity as power increases.
─────────────────────────────────────────
[head=3][bold]THREAT CAPABILITIES[/bold][/head]

[bold]Entropic Siphoning[/bold]  
[bullet] Cultists drain Entropy from living crew, causing slow health decay. Entropy fuels all higher‑order rituals and Monument empowerment.

[bold]Malign Influence[/bold]  
[bullet] Destabilization of the noosphere may cause the appearance of so-called Malign Rifts, or tears in the fabric of reality. Cultists may harvest these Rifts to empower themselves and their Monument.

[bold]Cosmic Transmutation[/bold]  
[bullet] Cultists may convert weapons and clothing into enchanted arms and armor.
─────────────────────────────────────────
[bold]Conversion Rituals[/bold]  
[bullet] Once the Monument is constructed, cultists can scribe glyphs to forcibly convert crew.  
[bullet] Mindshielded personnel are immune until the Monument reaches full power.  
[bullet] Failed conversions risk exposing the cult.
─────────────────────────────────────────
[bold]Atmospheric and Spatial Distortion[/bold]  
[bullet] Vacuous Spires rapidly consume local atmosphere and generate additional Entropy motes.
─────────────────────────────────────────
[bold]Final Ritual (“Ushering the End”)[/bold]  
[bullet] Once the Monument is fully empowered, the cult can perform a catastrophic ritual intended to summon an extra‑dimensional entity and destroy the station.
─────────────────────────────────────────
[head=3][bold]RECOMMENDED RESPONSE[/bold][/head]
[bullet] Conduct sweeps of maintenance areas for glyphs, runes, and monument construction.
[bullet] Detain individuals exhibiting trance‑like behavior or ritual involvement.
[bullet] Prevent cultists from gathering in groups.
[bullet] Neutralize the cult before it reaches full empowerment.
[bullet] Administer Holy Water to deconvert suspected cultists.
─────────────────────────────────────────
[head=3][bold]AUTHORIZED EQUIPMENT[/bold][/head]
[bullet] Lethal weaponry  
[bullet] Portable flashers and crowd‑control equipment  
[bullet] Holy water and equipment
